What happens on the real robot?

The target is chosen from task demonstrations. The policy jointly generates actions and forces; the visual force estimator does not score candidates at deployment. Only the selected end-effector action is sent to the robot. Force is used for selection, rather than sent as a force command. See Section III-D and Algorithm 1.
