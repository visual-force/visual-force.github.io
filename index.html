Experiments

Both methods use the same trained policy. The baseline executes one sampled action; VisualForce samples 32 action–force pairs and chooses the action whose predicted force is closest to a target from demonstrations.

Success rates: 15 trials per task and method. Full results in the paper ↗. Videos are sped up; clip lengths do not indicate task completion times.
